Data Privacy & Security Policy

1. Data Collection

Deliktas Geo-Analytics ("we", "our", "the system") only collects your email address and your continent preferences. We do not collect names, physical addresses, IP addresses, or any other personal identifiable information (PII).

2. Purpose of Use

Your email address is strictly used for one purpose: sending you automated satellite-based mega-fire alerts based on the regions you have selected.

3. Data Protection & Sharing

We take your privacy seriously. Your data is stored securely in our private database. We will never sell, rent, or share your email address with third parties, marketing agencies, or advertisers under any circumstances.

4. Your Rights (Right to be Forgotten)

You have full control over your data. You can completely remove your email address from our database at any time by using the "Unsubscribe" tab on our website. Once you unsubscribe, your record is permanently deleted from our active mailing list.

By subscribing to Deliktas Geo-Analytics, you confirm that you are at least 16 years of age (or the minimum legal age for digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors under 16.
